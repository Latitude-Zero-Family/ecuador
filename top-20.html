Top 20 places

20 places we plan to visit

Our list of the places we most want to see in Ecuador, from the Andes to the Galápagos. We'll mark each one as we go and write it up in the journal.

Your vote

Where should we go first?

Pick the place you think we should visit first. We'll announce the winner on Instagram at @latitudezerofamily.

0 of 20 visited
  1. 01

    Quito's Historic Center

    HIGHLANDS · Pichincha

    One of the best-preserved colonial centers in the Americas and the first city named a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Churches, plazas and rooftop views of the volcanoes.

    Our plan: Mostly flat plazas, but the streets are steep. Bring a carrier.

    Planned
  2. 02

    Mitad del Mundo

    HIGHLANDS · Pichincha

    The monument on the equator just north of Quito, plus the Intiñan museum where you can balance an egg on a nail.

    Our plan: A photo of Mateo with one foot in each hemisphere.

    Planned
  3. 03

    Cotopaxi National Park

    HIGHLANDS · Cotopaxi

    One of the world's highest active volcanoes, with a near-perfect snow cone, wild horses and the Limpiopungo lagoon.

    Our plan: High altitude. We'll stay lower and keep the visit short.

    Planned
  4. 04

    Quilotoa Lagoon

    HIGHLANDS · Cotopaxi

    A turquoise crater lake inside a collapsed volcano. The hike down is easy; the climb back up is not.

    Our plan: Viewpoint from the rim, and the hike when Mateo is older.

    Planned
  5. 05

    Otavalo Market

    HIGHLANDS · Imbabura

    The famous Saturday market of Kichwa textiles, weavings and crafts, plus the lakes and waterfalls around town.

    Our plan: Saturday morning, then lunch by Lago San Pablo.

    Planned
  6. 06

    Cuenca

    HIGHLANDS · Azuay

    A UNESCO-listed colonial city with blue-domed cathedrals, four rivers and a big expat community. Home of the Panama hat.

    Our plan: A strong contender for a longer stay.

    Planned
  7. 07

    Baños

    HIGHLANDS · Tungurahua

    The adventure capital, between the Andes and the Amazon. Hot springs, the Pailón del Diablo waterfall and the swing at the end of the world.

    Our plan: Waterfall trails and the hot springs, slowly.

    Planned
  8. 08

    Chimborazo

    HIGHLANDS · Chimborazo

    Ecuador's highest peak. Thanks to the equatorial bulge, its summit is the point on Earth farthest from its center.

    Our plan: Drive to the refuge, take the photo, come back down.

    Planned
  9. 09

    Mindo Cloud Forest

    HIGHLANDS · Pichincha

    Misty forest about two hours from Quito, full of hummingbirds, butterflies, waterfalls and chocolate farms.

    Our plan: High on our list for a first weekend trip.

    Planned
  10. 10

    Ingapirca

    HIGHLANDS · Cañar

    The largest Inca ruins in Ecuador, built around a Cañari site. The Temple of the Sun is the highlight.

    Our plan: An easy day trip from Cuenca.

    Planned
  11. 11

    El Cajas National Park

    HIGHLANDS · Azuay

    Wild páramo with hundreds of glacial lakes, about 45 minutes from Cuenca.

    Our plan: Short lakeside trails near the visitor center.

    Planned
  12. 12

    Guayaquil & the Malecón 2000

    COAST · Guayas

    Ecuador's largest city. The riverfront Malecón, Las Peñas neighborhood and the 444 steps up Cerro Santa Ana.

    Our plan: The Malecón is stroller-friendly. The 444 steps are on Austin.

    Planned
  13. 13

    Montañita

    COAST · Santa Elena

    The coast's famous surf town, laid-back by day and lively at night.

    Our plan: Daytime beach and surf lessons for Austin.

    Planned
  14. 14

    Puerto López & Isla de la Plata

    COAST · Manabí

    Base for humpback whale watching (roughly June to September) and a smaller-scale Galápagos with blue-footed boobies.

    Our plan: Timing the trip for whale season.

    Planned
  15. 15

    Los Frailes Beach

    COAST · Manabí

    A protected crescent beach inside Machalilla National Park, with no buildings in sight.

    Our plan: A calm-water beach day.

    Planned
  16. 16

    Tena & the Napo River

    AMAZON · Napo

    The gateway to the Amazon from the highlands. River trips, Kichwa communities and waterfalls.

    Our plan: A lodge stay close to town.

    Planned
  17. 17

    Cuyabeno Wildlife Reserve

    AMAZON · Sucumbíos

    Flooded forest and blackwater lagoons with pink river dolphins, monkeys and hundreds of bird species.

    Our plan: A trip for when Mateo is older.

    Planned
  18. 18

    Yasuní National Park

    AMAZON · Orellana

    One of the most biodiverse places on the planet. Deep-jungle lodges and parrot clay licks.

    Our plan: The big Amazon trip, someday.

    Planned
  19. 19

    Santa Cruz Island

    GALÁPAGOS

    The hub of the islands. Giant tortoises in the highlands, the Charles Darwin Research Station and Tortuga Bay.

    Our plan: Our first Galápagos base.

    Planned
  20. 20

    San Cristóbal Island

    GALÁPAGOS

    Sea lions on every bench, Kicker Rock and some of the best snorkeling in the islands.

    Our plan: Sea lions at eye level. Mateo will love it.

    Planned
