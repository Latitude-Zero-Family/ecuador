Food & recipes

Ecuador on a plate

What to eat, region by region, the family recipes Dennisse grew up on, and Mateo's first tastes of Ecuador.

What to eat

Dishes we can't wait to share

¡Qué rico!
COAST

Encebollado

A tuna and yuca soup with pickled red onion, eaten in the morning. Guayaquil's famous cure for everything.

¡Qué rico!
COAST

Bolón de verde

A fist-sized ball of mashed green plantain with cheese or pork crackling. The coastal breakfast.

¡Qué rico!
ANDES

Hornado

Slow-roasted whole pork with mote (hominy), potato patties, avocado and curtido. A highland market classic.

¡Qué rico!
ANDES

Llapingachos

Golden potato and cheese patties with chorizo, fried egg and peanut sauce. Ambato's pride.

¡Qué rico!
AMAZON

Maito

Fish wrapped in bijao leaves and cooked over the fire. Simple, smoky and very Amazon.

¡Qué rico!
SEASONAL

Fanesca

A rich soup of 12 grains and salt cod, made only during Holy Week. Every family's version is the best one.

¡Qué rico!
SEASONAL

Colada morada & guaguas de pan

A spiced purple corn and fruit drink with bread babies, made for Día de los Difuntos on November 2.
