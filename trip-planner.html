This is a starting point built from our own bucket list, not a booking. Check travel times, opening hours and current travel advice, and ask your pediatrician about altitude before you go.

  • Quito's Historic CenterOne of the best-preserved colonial centers in the Americas and the first city named a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  • Mitad del MundoThe monument on the equator just north of Quito, plus the Intiñan museum where you can balance an egg on a nail.
  • Otavalo MarketThe famous Saturday market of Kichwa textiles, weavings and crafts, plus the lakes and waterfalls around town.
  • Mindo Cloud ForestMisty forest about two hours from Quito, full of hummingbirds, butterflies, waterfalls and chocolate farms.
  • Cotopaxi National ParkOne of the world's highest active volcanoes, with a near-perfect snow cone, wild horses and the Limpiopungo lagoon.
  • Quilotoa LagoonA turquoise crater lake inside a collapsed volcano.
  • BañosThe adventure capital, between the Andes and the Amazon.
  • ChimborazoEcuador's highest peak.
  • Tena & the Napo RiverThe gateway to the Amazon from the highlands.
  • Cuyabeno Wildlife ReserveFlooded forest and blackwater lagoons with pink river dolphins, monkeys and hundreds of bird species.
  • Yasuní National ParkOne of the most biodiverse places on the planet.
  • CuencaA UNESCO-listed colonial city with blue-domed cathedrals, four rivers and a big expat community.
  • IngapircaThe largest Inca ruins in Ecuador, built around a Cañari site.
  • El Cajas National ParkWild páramo with hundreds of glacial lakes, about 45 minutes from Cuenca.
  • Guayaquil & the Malecón 2000Ecuador's largest city.
  • MontañitaThe coast's famous surf town, laid-back by day and lively at night.
  • Puerto López & Isla de la PlataBase for humpback whale watching (roughly June to September) and a smaller-scale Galápagos with blue-footed boobies.
  • Los Frailes BeachA protected crescent beach inside Machalilla National Park, with no buildings in sight.
  • Santa Cruz IslandThe hub of the islands.
  • San Cristóbal IslandSea lions on every bench, Kicker Rock and some of the best snorkeling in the islands.
